A considered process

What happens next.

Submitting an application does not automatically schedule a consultation. Every situation is reviewed individually.

  1. Application review

    SRG considers the tax, business, and financial context you provided.

  2. Clarification, if needed

    The team may follow up to understand a detail or decision more clearly.

  3. Secure document instructions

    When needed, SRG explains how to submit relevant documents securely.

  4. Team assessment

    Jay or the SRG team reviews the relevant information and documents.

  5. An appropriate next step

    Booking instructions are sent only after manual approval, when applicable.

Your documents deserve a secure process.

Please do not send tax returns, Social Security numbers, identification documents, or financial statements through ordinary email. Wait for SRG’s secure submission instructions.

Consultations are generally by phone after approval. Zoom or Microsoft Teams may be requested. In-person consultations require Jay’s approval, and a paid consultation may apply depending on the case.
